Quote Of The Day

Give your community something new to discuss every day with automated quote of the days and discussion threads.

Quote Of The Day Documentation

1 Command

Quote Of The Day Setup

Configure Quote of the Day by selecting a channel, choosing a role to ping and setting the send time.

/quote-of-the-day

Role Pings

Automatically mention a selected role whenever a new Quote of the Day is posted.

Discussion Threads

Every question automatically creates a Quote Of The Day Discussion thread so members can continue the conversation.

Immediate First Quote

When Quote Of The Day is enabled for the first time, Joinly immediately posts a quote Daily quotes will then continue at a time set by you.
