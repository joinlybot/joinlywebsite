QOTD Setup

Configure Question of the Day by selecting the channel, optional role to ping, and enabling the feature.

/qotd

Daily Questions

Joinly automatically posts a new question every day at 13:00 GMT.

Role Pings

Optionally mention a selected role whenever a new Question of the Day is posted.

Discussion Threads

Every question automatically creates a QOTD Discussion thread so members can continue the conversation.

Immediate First Question

When QOTD is enabled for the first time, Joinly immediately posts a question. Daily questions will then continue at 13:00 GMT.
